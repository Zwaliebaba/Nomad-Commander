CODEBASE LIMITS (AGENTS.md)

No blending (§5): gradients, glow, soft shadows and dashed anti-aliased lines must become flat fills, dithering or stepped rings. No camera or mesh (§5): the isometric view is fine only as precomputed integer positions. Font is 8×8 at integer scales; no icons (NC-026).
